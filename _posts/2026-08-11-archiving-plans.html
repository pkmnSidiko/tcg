Archiving Plans

August 11, 2026

Today is going to be one of those days where I'm goign to discuss the boring part of card collecting. While my cards are currently sorted by energy or trainer card type, that's just the start of the problem. I plan to start fresh on my scanning. For full(ish) binder pages I will be using PokeLenz, for planning master or other sets I will be using SetZen, and for my full collection I will be tracking in PriceCharting.

Now for my binders, I have four 09 pocket VaultX binders, three dedicated to multiple generations of Pokemon, while the fourth is my PC and Michi Method book. I also own a VaultX 04 pocket binder as my individual trade collection, with a seocndary 04 pocket binder from a generic Amazon seller that I use for bulk trade cards. You'll get more information on how I plan to use those books on my methodology page.

How I plan to scan through my sections is starting with the smallest group of cards and work my way up to the largest group. Which means starting with Fairy type cars, and working my way up to Grass and Psychic types. My pull luck is weird, I have more Grass and Psychic type cards, but not enough to play the game at all.

So wish me luck, I have a bit of a haul ahead of myself for the next few days.
