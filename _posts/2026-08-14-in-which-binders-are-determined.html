Field Journal

Let's Talk About Binders

August 14, 2026

For my pokedex project I wanted a good set of binders. My requirements were minimal. Side loading, zipper closure, sewn in pages. I've been burned too many times with traditional 3 ring binders.

I also wanted something I could customize as I went, and from what I saw on Pinterest and other social media with short form video focus, VaultX looked like a good brand. I am not adverse to paying more for a good quality product.

From there I ordered a few off of Amazon, and then found a coupon code to order drectly from VaultX--mainly as I wanted a purple binder for my Personal Collection/Michi Method book. I had also picked up some of their semi-rigid protectors at that time.

So now, I'm getting ready to pull everything out of each book, and to start fresh with my updated ruleset for my binders. The original plan was one generation per book, but that was overly excessive. I will be sharing video with the project after I figure out how I want to share it. Until then, I have to see how many of each pokemon I have.
