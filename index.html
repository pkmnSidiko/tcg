Welcome to the Archive

This is the public-facing archive of my trading card collection projects, binder systems, experiments, and assorted rabbit holes.

Pokémon TCG is the primary focus, but the archive also includes my other trading card collections and projects.

My collection is divided into several different systems. A master set, a Pokédex binder, a personal collection, and an artist collection don't necessarily follow the same rules.

Currently under construction!
There will probably be cards everywhere.
