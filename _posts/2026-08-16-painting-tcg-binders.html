Field Journal

Painting TCG Binders

August 16, 2026

This is just my first pass of my trade binder. While I'd love to give full credit for the base artwork, this seems to be some sort of Pokemon tattoo flash that is widely re-saved all over Pinterest. I will mention that I did add my own twist with the silver splash behind the artwork and the addition of Umbreon.

I am very happy with how this project is coming out, and am in the process of finalizing some of the fun additions that I want to include on the illustration end. After that part ist finished, I'm going to craft some nfc tags using Pokemon GO inspired keychains, ones that look like the Pokemon GO+ keychain from when the app was first release. I also plan on 3D printing those elements and then utilizing my nail polish collection to jazz up the finished 3D prints.

Since this first one is a smaller VaultX binder, only four pockets per side of the page; the layout and design for this painted element was intentionally smaller. The other binders will be much more involved. If you want a list of materials please feel free to let me know.

Other covers are still being designed, and while that is going on I am 3d printing a slightly smaller than life Umbreon for Halloween...

I'll be dressing up as my Pokemon Trainer/Researcher character this year! We're a Teal Pumpkin house so I also need to get my Halloween themed fidgets ready to print, and supplement with mochi squishies and other non-food treat.

Thankfully I have some time to get stuff done. See you next time.
